# Geophysics Workbench 0.7.0: usability round 1

The plugin should now feel more like part of QGIS: QGIS wording and conventions, undo for
everything you draw, more room for the section, and no overlapping labels. Based on a
hands-on usability review of 0.6.0.

## What's new

**Drawing like QGIS**
- Every piece and note you draw is on **Undo**. **Ctrl+Z** / **Ctrl+Y** (and Ctrl+Shift+Z)
  work while the section panel has focus.
- **Right-click finishes** a line or polygon (Esc cancels, Backspace removes the last point).
  Each tool shows a one-line hint how to use it.
- Dragging with the **Note** tool shows the stretch while you drag.
- **Frozen versions** are drawn in their QGIS layer style instead of a solid default fill.

**More room for the section**
- **Vertical exaggeration "Auto"** (the new default) fills the panel: a docked section now
  uses the whole width; the box shows what Auto chose, e.g. "Auto (1,4×)". Type a value to
  fix it as before.
- An empty panel says what to do, with a link to pick a profile on the map.
- The **layer list** is narrower (long names end in "…", full name on hover) and remembers
  the width you drag it to.
- **Measured data** starts hidden when the profile has a model, so its dots do not cover
  the model. Tick it to show it.
- **Labels never overlap** — on the section (also over sloping ground), in the borehole viewer
  (names stack in rows) and on plates. A label with no free room is left out; its column or
  mark stays, and hovering shows it. Station numbers are thinned to the space available.
- **Field-note stretches** lie in their own lanes just above the ground.
- **Numbers** follow QGIS's locale everywhere (24,5 m on a German system), never "−0".
- The readout under the section stays one line (full text on hover); before, a long readout
  widened the panel and the section seemed to zoom.

**Wording and visibility**
- QGIS wording: menu items and buttons in title case ("Context Layers…", "Zoom Full"),
  "…" on everything that opens a dialog, a tooltip on every tool button.
- Greyed-out menu entries say why ("Open a profile in the section panel first").
- Error messages name the file and say what to do next, without internal names.

**Bigger changes**
- **Context Layers…** has four tabs — *Lines*, *Points*, *Boreholes*, *Band and DEM* — and
  fits a laptop screen; it remembers its size. Plain labels ("Search distance from the line
  (m)"), column headers for point layers.
- **Rename and delete profiles**: right-click the profile list in the section panel, or the
  plugin menu. Deleting asks first and lists what goes with it; your data files and layers
  are never touched, and frozen interpretation versions keep their pieces.
- A **Pick on Map** button sits next to the profile list.
- **Colour ramps from QGIS** in Display…: QGIS's ramp button with its ramps and your own.
  Existing projects and plates keep exactly their colours until you pick a ramp. Contour
  settings are only active when contours are drawn; their spacing shows "decades" on a
  logarithmic scale.

**Plates (from testing 0.6.0)**
- Finer standard scales and VEs, so a section fills its frame; the depth always fits.
- Compact colour bars; a working mini-map scale bar; room for labels above the ground.

## What to try

1. Open the Erfurt demo (`demo_erfurt/erfurt_project.qgz`) and open **ERT02** in the section
   panel: it fills the panel at Auto VE; the core labels sit in rows without overlaps.
2. Draw a line (click, click, **right-click**), then press **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y**.
3. Drag a stretch with the **Note** tool: the stretch shows while you drag.
4. Right-click the profile list → **Rename Profile…**.
5. **Context Layers…**: the four tabs on a laptop screen.
6. A layer's colour button → **Display…** → pick a QGIS colour ramp.

## Known limits

- While a QGIS layer is being edited, QGIS's own Undo also listens to Ctrl+Z; then Ctrl+Z in
  the section panel may do nothing. Use the panel's Undo and Redo buttons.

- Deleting a profile cannot be undone (it asks first).
- Notes edited in the Field Notes… list are not on the panel's undo stack.
- The coverage threshold in Display… has no unit (it is the reader's own coverage measure).
