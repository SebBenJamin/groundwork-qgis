# Groundwork 0.14.1: Calhoun Line 6 in its place

A fix of the Calhoun demo; the plugin itself is unchanged.

## Line 6 stands where it was measured

In the Calhoun demo of 0.13 and 0.14.0, **Line 6** stood about **23 m too early** along its
own track. Its surface, from the authors' LiDAR heights, therefore missed the elevation
model by up to 12 m: in the section, the terrain line ran beside the surface; in the 3D
view, the curtain lagged behind the terrain.

The cause was in the demo's build: it placed each line along the authors' map points of
that line, and for Line 6 those points sit about 23 m off. The build now checks every line
against the 1 m elevation model and moves it to where its own heights fit. Line 6 moves by
23 m and now fits to 0.4 m; lines 10, 11 and 12 move by at most 2.5 m. The demo's
`ATTRIBUTION.md` says so.

## Also changed

- The Calhoun elevation model is marked as an **elevation surface** (Layer Properties →
  Elevation), as Gülpe's DGM1 is since 0.14.0.

## Updating

Download `groundwork_geophysics-demo-calhoun-0.14.1.zip` and unzip it into a new folder.
If you drew on the old Calhoun demo, keep that folder: your drawings sit on the old Line 6.
The plugin zip is the same as 0.14.0 apart from its version number; installing it is
optional.
