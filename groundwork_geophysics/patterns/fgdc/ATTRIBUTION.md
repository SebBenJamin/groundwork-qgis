# FGDC lithology patterns

The SVG files in this folder are the sedimentary (600-series) and the metamorphic, igneous and vein-matter (700-series) lithology patterns of the

**FGDC Digital Cartographic Standard for Geologic Map Symbolization, FGDC-STD-013-2006**,
prepared by the U.S. Geological Survey for the Federal Geographic Data Committee
(<https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/fgdc_gds/geolsymstd.php>).

- As a work of the United States federal government, the symbology is in the public domain.
- The SVG files come from Daven Quinn's extraction of the pattern library, [davenquinn/geologic-patterns](https://github.com/davenquinn/geologic-patterns), commit `7fdb820b024395f140bd6edb60c99f6001458364`. That repackaging is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>).
- The drawings are unchanged. The files are renamed from `607.svg` to `607 Massive sand or sandstone.svg` (code and name from the standard's pattern chart, characters not allowed in file names left out). `index.csv` lists code, file and name.
- The patterns are drawn in black. Put a coloured simple fill under the SVG fill in a QGIS symbol for a coloured background.
