# Geophysics Workbench 0.4.0: models shown properly

Your inversion models can now look like the figure in your report. Draw a model as its
cells, as a smooth colour field, as labelled contours, or as both field and contours. Where
the inversion's coverage is low, the model can fade out. The section panel is also tidier.

## What's new

- **Display…** for each model layer. It opens from a click on the layer's colour-scale
  button, or from right-click → **Display…** in the layer list. One dialog holds:
  - **Colour scale:** as before.
  - **Draw as:**
    - **Cells:** as inverted.
    - **Smooth colour field.**
    - **Contours.**
    - **Smooth field + contours.**
  - **Grid cell:** automatic, or your own size in metres.
  - **Contours:** automatic (1-2-5 per decade for resistivity, round steps otherwise), or
    your own spacing in decades or units. Labels sit on the lines.
  - **Coverage:** "Fade where coverage is low", below a threshold you set; Fade or Blank.
    - It's off by default.
    - It's available when the model file carries coverage or sensitivity: BERT/pyGIMLi
      `coverage(log10)` and ResIPy `Sensitivity(log10)`. The Gülpe models have it.
- **The smooth field stays inside the model.** It never paints above the ground, outside
  the mesh, or into cells without a value.
  - Values are averaged onto the mesh corners and blended within each cell. The field is
    smooth, so a single small cell's extreme value is softened; the readout still gives it.
- **Blank and Fade apply to contours too.** Blanked parts have no contour lines. Faded parts
  show faint lines without labels.
- **The readout and click details still report the model's own cell values**, whatever the
  display mode.
- **The faded edge is a snap target** while drawing. To mark an "unreliable" zone along it,
  draw it yourself; nothing is generated automatically.
- **Compact interpretation toolbar:**
  - The drawing tools, Snap, Undo and Redo are now icons.
  - Types…, Export…, Freeze…, "Start from this version" and **Compare with** are in the
    **Interpretation** menu.
  - When you view a frozen version, the Version box is tinted and its tooltip says it is
    read-only.

## What to try

1. Open the Gülpe demo, then Transect 3.
2. Click the layer's colour-scale button and choose **Smooth field + contours**.
3. In the same dialog, tick **Fade where coverage is low** and move the threshold.
4. Choose the **Polygon** tool and draw along the faded edge. With Snap on, points snap to
   the edge.
5. Hover the smooth field. The readout gives the cell value, the same as in Cells mode.

## Known limits

- Display settings last for the QGIS session, like the colour scales.
- Contour labels: one per line, at its middle, and skipped on short lines.
- At most 100 contour levels: a very small spacing is widened automatically.
- The fade edge follows the display grid, so it can look slightly stepped.
- R2 `.dat` models have no coverage, so their Coverage section is greyed out. Use ResIPy's
  `.vtk` export for coverage.
