# Geophysics Workbench 0.8.0: round 2

Fixes and small features from testing 0.7.0: contours at the levels you choose, a soft
coverage fade, boreholes you can leave out of one section, field notes styled like any QGIS
layer, a title on every plate section, and interpretation features with a name instead of a
letter.

## What's new

**Display**

- **Contour levels you type**: in **Display…** (contours part) enter **Major levels**, e.g.
  `10;30;100;300`, and **Minor levels**, e.g. `20;50;200`, in the model's unit (Ω·m). Major
  lines are labelled, minor lines are thinner and unlabelled. Leave both empty for the
  automatic levels as before. Plates draw the same contours. A value that is not a number,
  or lies outside the colour scale, is named in the dialog.
- The **coverage fade** follows the smoothed model softly in the smooth modes; no more
  blocky edge. The cells mode is unchanged.

**Boreholes**

- **Hide a borehole in one profile**: right-click its column in the section → **Hide in
  Section**. It leaves that profile's section and plates; the map, the other profiles and
  your borehole layer are not touched. **Ctrl+Z** brings it back; so does a right-click
  anywhere else in the section → **Show Hidden Boreholes (n)**.
- The **borehole viewer** still shows hidden boreholes, faded and marked "hidden in section".
  Click a column to select it, Ctrl+click to select several, right-click → **Hide in Section**
  or **Show in Section**. **Show › Hidden boreholes** switches them off in the viewer.

**Field notes**

- Notes in the section take their **colour from the QGIS layers** "Field notes" (notes at one
  point) and "Field notes (stretches)", and a stretch its line width too. Style them in Layer
  Properties like any layer; categorized styles colour each note by its category. Changes show
  at once.
- When a project has notes, the two layers are added to the project, so there is always a
  layer to style. Existing note layers get the workbench look once.
- The **Note tool** keeps the cursor linked to the map, also while you drag a stretch.

**Interpretation**

- A feature has **Type, Name, Confidence and Note**. **Name** replaces the letter: any text,
  e.g. "Clay A" or "Quaternary sand", drawn as the label. Your letters become names. The
  "already used" hint only looks at the features on the same profile.
- Restyling an interpretation layer in QGIS shows in the section at once.

**Plates**

- Each section on a plate has a **title**, the profile's name, at the top of its frame.
  Edit it in the Layout Designer; Update keeps it.
- **Update keeps your edits** of every text the plugin writes: plate title, coordinate
  system line, title-block captions, field-notes list and section titles, also those you
  edited in 0.7. When you edited the field-notes list and the notes then change, the message
  after Update says so.

**Fixes**

- **Patterns…** opens at once (it took seconds with many classes).
- Switching on the measured layer no longer changes the colours of a crossing profile's strip.
- After placing a note, all workbench layers on the map are repainted. Profile lines
  sometimes vanished from the map after a note was placed; this could not be reproduced
  here, so the repaint is a safeguard. Please tell us if it still happens.

## Your files are updated once

The first time 0.8 opens a workbench file (`*_workbench.gpkg`), it renames the interpretation
field `letter` to `name` and updates the interpretation layers' QGIS styles in the project
(labels, rules). After that, **0.7 can no longer open the file**. Keep a copy if you need to
go back. A file that cannot be written (read-only, open in another program) is not opened;
the message says what to do.

## What to try

1. Open a model in **Display…** → Draw as *Smooth field + contours* → Major levels
   `10;100`, Minor levels `20;50;200`.
2. Right-click a borehole column in the section → **Hide in Section**; then open the borehole
   viewer and bring it back with right-click → **Show in Section**.
3. Restyle "Field notes (stretches)" in Layer Properties: the section follows at once.
4. Edit an interpretation feature: the form asks for Type, **Name**, Confidence and Note.
5. **Plates…** → Create / Update: each frame has its title; edit one in the Layout Designer
   and update again.

## Known limits

- **Two sections on an A4 plate** can come out badly (a very small scale; the model below
  the frame, or colour-bar ticks over the provenance lines). This was already so in 0.7; the
  new title strip makes it a little worse. One section per A4 plate, or A3, works.
- The first interpretation edit on a profile adds the interpretation layers to the project,
  and the section switches to their styles (as since 0.3).

- Expressions **outside** the workbench's interpretation layers that use the field
  `letter` (e.g. in your own layers or layouts) must be changed to `name` by hand.
- An older plugin (0.7) cannot open a workbench file updated by 0.8.
- In the section, notes take the colour of their symbol; the map label colour of the
  "Field notes" layer does not change the section.
