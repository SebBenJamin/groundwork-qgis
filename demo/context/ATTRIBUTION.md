Powerline geometry fetched via the Overpass API; reprojected to EPSG:25832.

pylons.geojson is derived from the same OSM powerline: its vertices inside the demo area,
which in OSM are the towers (same licence and attribution).

Everything else in this folder is synthetic, made up by scripts/build_demo.py for the demo:
boreholes.gpkg (boreholes and logs), geology.geojson (surface geology) and dem.tif
(terrain with a road embankment). None of it describes the real ground at this place.
